PlayGate Web Control

Sends PlayGate v2 input frames to the ESP32-C6 gateway over Wi-Fi (WebSocket) or USB (Web Serial). A physical gamepad recognised by the browser drives the gateway directly; the on-screen controls take over whenever the pad is at rest. Latency is shown as reported by the device and by this page; judge smoothness by feel.
